Retail marketplace · open provider network

Pick your plan. Pick your provider.

The Power Store is the retail face of the open Axiom provider network — where a customer compares real plans from more than one provider side by side and picks their own, instead of shopping one reseller's brand. It runs on the same Axiom Retail and Axiom Market layers as The Power Place, just pointed outward instead of white-labeled.

Compare plans See The Power Place →

Where things stand: Oklahoma does not yet authorize residential retail electric choice, so The Power Store is a concept preview of the multi-provider marketplace ahead of that launch. Provider names and plans below are illustrative placeholders, not real offers — live provider listings appear once network partners are onboarded to Axiom Market.
