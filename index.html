Build order

Storefront first. Everything else is plumbing.

The Power Place ships in the order a reseller actually needs it — a branded storefront your customers see, a provider network that stocks it, and an exchange API wiring it together underneath, built to eventually reach out to bigger networks.

FIRST — RESELLER STOREFRONT

Storefront

A fully branded shopping and enrollment flow — your name, your colors, your rate plans — dropped into your site or handed to customers as a hosted page. This is the only layer your customers ever see, so it ships first.

Runs on Axiom Retail
NEXT — PROVIDER NETWORK

Provider Network

Stock the storefront with your own supply, a partner REP, or the open Axiom network of plans from other providers — so there's something to sell the moment the storefront goes live.

Runs on Axiom Market
LAST — EXCHANGE API

Exchange API

The settlement and reconciliation backend underneath both — built to eventually interoperate with larger retail platforms such as Octopus Energy's Kraken. It ships last, once the storefront and provider layers are proven.

Runs on Axiom Exchange
How it works

From signed reseller to live storefront.

Step 1

Brand the storefront

Send your logo, colors and plan names. This ships first because it's the whole product to your customers — everything behind it stays invisible.

Step 2

Stock it

Connect your own supply, a partner REP, or the open Axiom provider network — pricing and eligibility flow through automatically either way.

Step 3

Wire the backend

The exchange API settles and reconciles everything underneath, with a roadmap to plug into larger networks like Octopus Energy's Kraken. It ships last, once the first two layers are proven.

Step 4

Go live

Launch to a pilot cohort under your own name, watch performance on the same dashboards Veritris Energy uses in Texas, then scale.
